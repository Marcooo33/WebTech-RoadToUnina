Reach Federico II

Start from a random Italian Wikipedia article and navigate — using only the internal links inside each article — to Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II.

Play for the fewest clicks (CLICK mode) or the lowest effective time (TIME mode), across three difficulty levels that change how much you can backtrack.

How to play

  • Every game starts on a random article and ends when you reach the target page.
  • Only valid internal article links count as a move; the backend decides what is valid.
  • EASY: unlimited backtracking. MEDIUM: 3 backtracks. HARD: none.
  • Backtracking never counts as a click.
  • Games are saved on the server — leave and resume any time, from any device.
